The Clearline promise

Repair or replace?

Straight answers, not sales pitches.

Our technicians show you both options in writing and explain the trade-offs. Here's the same rule of thumb we use:

01

Under 10 years old?

If the repair is reasonable, we'll almost always recommend fixing it. A newer system has plenty of life left.

02

The $5,000 rule

Multiply the system's age by the repair cost. Over $5,000 — replacement usually makes more financial sense.

03

Still running R-22 refrigerant?

R-22 production ended in the U.S. in 2020, so major repairs get expensive. We'll show you the real numbers.

FAQ

Good questions.

Still unsure? Call (512) 555-0176 and talk to a real person.

Can you really come the same day?

Most weekdays, yes — when you book before 2pm we can usually get a technician to you that afternoon. In peak heat waves we'll tell you honestly when we can be there.

How much does an AC repair cost?

It depends on the part and the system. After the diagnostic visit you get one flat, written price. You decide whether to go ahead — no pressure and no hourly billing.

How do I know if I should repair or replace my AC?

Age, repair cost and refrigerant type matter most. Our technicians show you both options in writing and recommend the one that makes sense for you, even if it's the cheaper one.

How long does a new AC installation take?

Most replacements are completed in a single day. Larger or more complex systems can take two.

Are your technicians licensed?

Yes. Air conditioning work in Texas requires a TACLA license from the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation, and our license number is shown at the bottom of every page.

What should I do while I wait for the technician?

Check that the thermostat is set to Cool, replace a dirty filter if you have one, and make sure the outdoor unit's breaker hasn't tripped. If you see ice on the lines, turn the system off.
